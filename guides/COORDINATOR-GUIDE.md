# Rockets College Recruiting: coordinator guide

One page for club staff (recruiting coordinators and team coaches). Use it on a computer or a phone.

## 1. Getting access

1. Sign up in the app like a family does (with the shared sign-up code), using your own email.
2. Tell the club administrator. They switch your account to staff and assign your athletes. Sign out and in again; you now see a top bar: **Roster, Find Colleges, Account**.
3. You see only the athletes assigned to you. If one is missing, ask the administrator.

## 2. Roster

Each card shows team, class, profile %, number of colleges, last activity, and a red **N overdue** badge. Search by the start of a name; sort by most overdue, longest without activity, or least complete profile. Start your week with "Most overdue".

## 3. Working inside an athlete's account

Tap an athlete. A yellow banner says **"Editing <athlete>'s account as <you>"**: everything you change is recorded under your name, and the family sees "added by <your name>". Tabs:

| Tab | What it is |
|---|---|
| Home | Their overdue and due-this-week steps, quiet colleges, recent activity |
| List | Their colleges; open one for Overview, Activity and Contacts |
| Find | Search colleges and add them to *their* list |
| Profile | Personal, Athletic, Academics, Highlights; each tab saves on its own |
| Preferences | Their college preferences |
| History | Who changed what and when (filter: staff changes only) |

Good practice:

- Set a **next step with a due date** on every active college; that is what drives the Home page and the roster's overdue count.
- Log calls and emails you handle for the athlete; the entry is labelled as yours.
- Agree with the family before changing their priorities or stages; they see your changes.
- The 30-college limit and one open step per college apply to you too; the screen explains if you hit one.

## 4. Keeping the college data right (all athletes see it)

Open any college (**Find Colleges** tab or inside an athlete) and scroll to **Staff tools**:

- **Edit college information**: program page, questionnaire, camps, Instagram, X, last season, college site, cost page, and **program status**. Use *Needs review* when unsure; *Program dropped* hides the program from search.
- **Mark as verified today** when you have checked a page and it is right.
- **Coaches (shared records)**: edit a coach, add a coach, mark a coach as *Left the program* (kept, but hidden from families), and tick **Main recruiting contact** (moving it clears the previous one).
- Every edit records your name and the date. Please do at least one real correction in your first week.

You cannot change enrolment, tuition or admission figures; those come from the data files.

## 5. Limits and reminders

- Everything in the app is about minors. Do not copy, screenshot, email or print athlete information outside the club. Sign out on shared computers.
- You cannot delete athletes or change who is assigned; ask the administrator.
- Contact details can be stale; the "last verified" date on each college shows how old they are.
