# Rockets College Recruiting: family guide

One page for athletes and parents. Works on a phone; add the site to your home screen for one-tap access.

## 1. Get started (5 minutes)

1. Open the site link the club sent you.
2. **Create account**: your name, an email you check, a password (8 or more characters), and the **sign-up code** the club gave you. Tick the box to confirm you have read the notice. If sign-up fails, check the code and try again; ask your coordinator if it still fails.
3. **Create your athlete profile**: name, date of birth, graduation year, home state. Athletes must be 14 to 18.
4. Open **Profile** and fill in the four tabs (Personal, Athletic, Academics, Highlights). Each tab has its own **Save** button. The bar at the top shows how complete your profile is; coaches decide fast, so aim for 100%.
5. Open **More → My College Preferences** and tell us what you are looking for (division, level, size, setting, majors, deal-breakers). This powers "Match my preferences" in Find.

## 2. Build your list

- **Find**: search by college name and use Filters (division, state, level, size, setting, selectivity, major). Tap **Match my preferences** to apply what you saved.
- Tap a college to see its facts, volleyball program links, the questionnaire and the coaches' contacts (tap an email or phone to use it, **Copy** to copy).
- **Add to My List.** The limit is 30. A good plan: start with 20 to 30, then narrow to the 10 to 15 you will really contact.
- Contact details change. Always check the program page before you write.

## 3. Work your list

- **My List**: your colleges, filtered by priority and stage, sorted by priority, longest quiet, or next step due.
- Open a college:
  - **Overview**: set priority (1 top choice to 4 backup) and stage (researching, shortlisted, contacted, in conversation, camp or visit, offer, committed, closed), pick your main coach, tick the recruiting questionnaire when done, and write why you like it. **Remove from My List** keeps the history, and you can restore it.
  - **Activity**: **Log** every email, call, text, camp or visit, with the date. Flag "I need to follow up" to set a next step. Entries marked *Automatic* (stage changes, added or removed colleges) are written by the app and cannot be edited. Entries *added by your coordinator* are labelled so you know who wrote them.
  - **Contacts**: the coaches' details, plus contacts you add yourself (only you and your coordinators see those).
- Every college can have **one next step** with a due date (for example "Email Coach Lee, due Friday"). When you finish it, the app offers to log it and set the next one.
- **Home** shows what needs attention: overdue steps, steps due this week, colleges with no contact for 21 days, and recent activity.

## 4. Who can see what

- Your club coordinators can see and edit your profile, list and log. Their name is recorded on anything they change, and you see "added by <name>".
- Other families cannot see your information. College coaches cannot see it either: the app does not send anything to colleges. You contact coaches yourself, by email or phone.
- Do not share your password. Sign out on shared devices (**More → Sign out**).

## 5. If something goes wrong

- **Forgot your password**: on the sign-in page, tap *Forgot password* and follow the email. If no email arrives, ask your coordinator.
- **Change password**: More → Change password.
- **"Your session ended"**: sign in again; nothing is lost.
- **A page will not load**: check your connection and tap *Try again*; if it keeps failing, reload the page.
- **Wrong or missing college information**: tell your coordinator; they can correct it for everyone.
- **Delete my account and data**: ask your coordinator or the club administrator.
